      How it works

      Training images were collected online (Wikimedia Commons, Flickr – open licences, source and licence recorded for every image) and labelled per class. Three models are compared: Random Forest / XGBoost on handcrafted features, a fine-tuned MobileNetV2 CNN, and a hybrid (CNN features + XGBoost). Photos that are not waste are reported as "not sure" instead of being forced into a class.
